# Soft Skills and Processes to study

The non-technical part of the Level Up career plan. The technical skills are in [[The Best Notes of the F Word#From the Level Up career plan (Senior Software Engineer)]].

> [!warning]- Read this first: the page status is not what I know
> The plan marks almost all of these as **Developed skill**, but that is only the status of the plan, not what is studied. A topic counts only when it is written in this vault. **None** of the skills below is in these notes, so all of them are here.
> **Delegation** was the only soft skill that was removed from this list, because it is already answered in [[Assessment Questions#How do you delegate a task properly, and what can be delegated?]].

> [!question] Why this matters for the assessment
> The fourth expert of the panel asks **management and situational** questions ([[Assessment Questions#4. Management & situational]]). The "What you should know" of every skill below is the **evaluation criteria** they use, so it is a good list of the behaviours they want to hear in the answer.

> [!tip] How to answer a soft skill question
> Never answer with a definition. Answer with a **situation → what I did → result**. The criteria below tell you which behaviours to put inside that story.

## Communication and conflict

### Communication
https://levelup.epam.com/skill/skillId=7770000000000108086&skillLevelId=7770000000000001002&externalUserId=8760000000007062455&planId=0f1fde13-8c31-464f-b6d4-a40d1aee42df
Nivel objetivo: Intermediate (self-declared: **not specified** — this one is empty in the plan).
Resumen: express ideas clearly and build a real dialogue, in person and online.
Topics clave a dominar:
- Communicate effectively inside the team **and beyond** it
- Build a dialogue: listen actively and clarify
- Give **and receive** constructive feedback
- Business correspondence, written effectively and diplomatically
- Facilitate a team meeting

### Conflict management
https://levelup.epam.com/skill/skillId=7770000000000110051&skillLevelId=7770000000000001002&externalUserId=8760000000007062455&planId=0f1fde13-8c31-464f-b6d4-a40d1aee42df
Nivel objetivo: Intermediate (self-declared: **not specified**). The page shows it as **pending**.
Resumen: identify and handle a conflict in a fair and efficient way.
Topics clave a dominar:
- **Prevent** the conflict: encourage open conversation, stop a confrontation that is not productive
- Move from the emotions to the **subject** of the conflict
- Identify the **real interests** of the other person, not only their position
- Look for **win/win** solutions, orientation to cooperation and consensus
- Avoid the escalation in most of the situations

### Negotiations
https://levelup.epam.com/skill/skillId=7770000000000109949&skillLevelId=7770000000000001001&externalUserId=8760000000007062455&planId=0f1fde13-8c31-464f-b6d4-a40d1aee42df
Nivel objetivo: Novice.
Resumen: explore alternatives and bring good arguments to get other people on board.
Topics clave a dominar:
- Understand our **own** interests and needs first
- Set clear goals for the negotiation and keep the focus on them
- **Anticipate the arguments** of the other side
- Generate several solutions and explain their value **for the opponent**
- Adapt to the patterns of the country or the culture of the project

### Being a Team Player
https://levelup.epam.com/skill/skillId=7770000000002740632&skillLevelId=7770000000000001002&externalUserId=8760000000007062455&planId=0f1fde13-8c31-464f-b6d4-a40d1aee42df
Nivel objetivo: Intermediate (self-declared: **not specified**). The page shows it as **pending**.
Resumen: work cooperatively in a diverse team.
Topics clave a dominar:
- Transparent and empathetic communication, with respect and tact
- Act as it was agreed in the team
- Complete the shared tasks in time
- Adaptability and flexibility to work effectively
- Initiative in problem-solving, contribute ideas and opinions

### Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion
https://levelup.epam.com/skill/skillId=7770000000000102400&skillLevelId=7770000000000001002&externalUserId=8760000000007062455&planId=0f1fde13-8c31-464f-b6d4-a40d1aee42df
Nivel objetivo: Intermediate.
Resumen: create an inclusive environment and work well across cultures.
Topics clave a dominar:
- How the culture changes the way people **communicate, perceive and transfer** a message
- Interpret verbal and non-verbal communication in a culturally correct way
- Be sensitive to the different norms of politeness
- Identify the expectations about the communication and **agree common rules** with the team
- Bias awareness and cultural intelligence

## Leadership and developing people

### Leadership
https://levelup.epam.com/skill/skillId=7770000000000113303&skillLevelId=7770000000000001001&externalUserId=8760000000007062455&planId=0f1fde13-8c31-464f-b6d4-a40d1aee42df
Nivel objetivo: Novice.
Resumen: influence and guide people towards a common goal.
Topics clave a dominar:
- Emerge as a leader because of the **feedback of the peers and the managers**, not because of the title
- Direct the group to a common goal
- Take responsibility for the **outcome of the team**, not only for our own tasks
- Organize the teamwork and engage people in collaborative initiatives

### Managing Teamwork
https://levelup.epam.com/skill/skillId=7770000000000104076&skillLevelId=7770000000000001001&externalUserId=8760000000007062455&planId=0f1fde13-8c31-464f-b6d4-a40d1aee42df
Nivel objetivo: Novice.
Resumen: lead a team to shared goals with the Hungry, Humble and Smart principles.
Topics clave a dominar:
- The three behaviours: **Hungry, Humble, Smart** (they can ask for them by the name)
- Clarify responsibilities, tasks and expectations
- Encourage the open sharing of information, so everybody stays aligned
- Recognize cultural and communication differences, and ask for guidance when it is needed

### Motivation
https://levelup.epam.com/skill/skillId=7770000000000100260&skillLevelId=7770000000000001001&externalUserId=8760000000007062455&planId=0f1fde13-8c31-464f-b6d4-a40d1aee42df
Nivel objetivo: Novice.
Resumen: keep the effort of the people aligned with the goals of the team.
Topics clave a dominar:
- Know the **needs, aspirations and strengths** of every person, and assign the tasks with that
- Help the person to see their **personal benefit** of doing the job well
- Make people feel that their work is important
- Share the ownership and the visibility, do not keep it

### Developing Others
https://levelup.epam.com/skill/skillId=7770000000000100655&skillLevelId=7770000000000001001&externalUserId=8760000000007062455&planId=0f1fde13-8c31-464f-b6d4-a40d1aee42df
Nivel objetivo: Novice.
Resumen: grow the people around us and address the performance problems.
Topics clave a dominar:
- Address a performance problem **proactively**, looking for the root cause together
- Talk about career aspirations and feed the **PDP** (Personal Development Plan)
- Encourage a growth mindset, share the opportunities of the company
- Prepare a person for their **assessment** with constructive feedback
- Related: [[Assessment Questions#How do you delegate a task properly, and what can be delegated?]] — delegate what makes the person grow

### Driving Change and Innovation
https://levelup.epam.com/skill/skillId=7770000000000110989&skillLevelId=7770000000000001001&externalUserId=8760000000007062455&planId=0f1fde13-8c31-464f-b6d4-a40d1aee42df
Nivel objetivo: Novice.
Resumen: guide people through a change and bring new ways of working.
Topics clave a dominar:
- Promote the understanding of the change and **address the resistance** to it
- Suggest change paths when the current approach does not work any more
- Spot the cases where the risk is high and something has to change
- Challenge the status quo, try approaches out of the standard procedure
- Analyse an innovative idea, see if it has potential, and plan how to operationalize it

## Processes and methodologies

### Software Engineering Processes
https://levelup.epam.com/skill/skillId=7770000000000109664&skillLevelId=7770000000000001003&externalUserId=8760000000007062455&planId=0f1fde13-8c31-464f-b6d4-a40d1aee42df
Nivel objetivo: Advanced — the highest of all the non-technical skills.
Resumen: choose, establish and improve the development process, the requirements and the estimations.
Topics clave a dominar:
- **Establish** a development process, or improve one that already exists
- Key metrics of the project and of the methodology, and how to keep the **predictability** of the delivery with them
- Which decision we take when a metric goes wrong
- How to **improve an estimation** after an under-estimation or an over-estimation
- Plan and estimate a significant scope (a component, a layer, a module)
- **Secure SDLC**: establish secure development practices in a team and support them
- Work with requirements, and identify + control the non-functional ones
- Already covered in the vault: [[Assessment Questions#What estimation techniques have you used?]], [[Assessment Questions#When does waterfall work better than agile?]], [[Assessment Questions#What are non-functional requirements? Name some]]

## English

### English B2
https://levelup.epam.com/skill/skillId=7770000000000104932&skillLevelId=7770000000000001014&externalUserId=8760000000007062455&planId=0f1fde13-8c31-464f-b6d4-a40d1aee42df
Nivel objetivo: B2.
Resumen: the level of English required by the plan, and the assessment is in English.
Topics clave a dominar:
- Understand the main ideas of a complex text, including **technical discussions** of our specialization
- Interact with fluency and spontaneity with a proficient speaker
- Produce detailed texts, combining and evaluating information from different sources
- Explain a viewpoint giving the **advantages and the disadvantages** of the options
- Describe a **complex process** out loud — this is exactly what the technical questions ask for
- Write a report or an article developing an argument with reasons
